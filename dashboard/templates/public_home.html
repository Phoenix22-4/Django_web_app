What AquaSavvy Does

AquaSavvy Solution connects smart level sensors and pump controls to a secure cloud dashboard. Our system continuously reads water levels, analyzes trends, and triggers safe pump actions when needed. You get instant visibility, alerts when tanks are low or full, and simple controls to manage your pump remotely.
